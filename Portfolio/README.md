# Pune Paneer Safety & Supply-Chain Risk Analysis

**Independent Business Analyst portfolio case study**  
**Research cut-off:** 26 September 2026

## Business problem

Pune has seen repeated food-safety enforcement actions involving paneer and dairy products. The objective of this case study is to organize public evidence, identify supply-chain/control gaps, distinguish enforcement evidence from market prevalence, and propose a restaurant-level quality-control framework.

## Core insight

The public-data gap is itself a business-analysis finding: public sources do not provide a representative Pune dataset linking restaurant tier, paneer supplier, batch, sample and final laboratory outcome. This project therefore does **not** invent restaurant-tier risk percentages.

## Key evidence

- 4 Pune enforcement events are included (2023, 2025 and two in 2026).
- 14,315 kg of paneer is the sum of quantities reported/seized across those 4 cited events. This is **not** a city-wide prevalence measure.
- A Maharashtra surveillance result reported 109 non-conforming samples out of 308 analysed paneer/dairy-analogue samples for Apr 2025-Mar 2026 (35.4%). This is a **statewide benchmark, not a Pune-specific rate**.
- Maharashtra's FDA lists a one-year prohibition on analogue/non-dairy paneer.
- FSSAI provides standards for paneer and citizen mechanisms for complaint / licence verification.

## Proposed future-state control model

Supplier qualification -> FSSAI licence verification -> invoice & batch traceability -> receiving checks -> risk-based laboratory testing -> supplier scorecard -> dashboard monitoring -> corrective action.

## Data integrity note

The evidence dataset is built from publicly reported enforcement events and official regulatory information. It is not a random sample of all paneer sold in Pune. Terms such as “suspected” and “samples sent for testing” are retained where source reporting uses those terms.
